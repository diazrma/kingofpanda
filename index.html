Card Scanner — free

Customers scan their credit card with the camera at checkout and the number, expiry date and name are filled in automatically, in any payment method that renders card fields on the page. OCR runs entirely in the browser; the image is never uploaded.

composer require kingofpanda/module-card-scanner
